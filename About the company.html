PROJECT HISTORY

The OWEEL car disc store arose as a response to the growing needs of car enthusiasts for high-quality, stylish and innovative solutions. The founders noticed that buyers are not just looking for wheels to improve the appearance of the car, but also appreciate safety, reliability and convenience of choice.

The idea was to create a single platform combining an online store with a user-friendly interface and an offline showroom, where you can personally familiarize yourself with the product, get expert advice and test the discs in action. This symbiosis has allowed us to set a new standard of service in the automotive industry.
